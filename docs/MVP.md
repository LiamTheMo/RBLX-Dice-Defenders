# MVP — Verdant Trial (first playable slice)

**Status:** build contract for v0.04. Numbers marked *tune* are placeholders for Studio playtests. Everything outside this file's scope is deferred (see end).

## Scope
- **Solo**, one match per server. Owner = first player whose profile loaded; if the owner leaves, the next loaded player becomes owner and the server returns to Lobby. Non-owners get `Phase = "NotOwner"` and every intent is rejected. Set place max players to 1 for production.
- **Deck:** the profile's equipped deck, exactly 5 distinct owned die types. The starter deck is C01 Pebble, C02 Needle, C03 Hammer, C04 Twinshot, C05 Longshot (stats/perks from DICE_CATALOG.md; Hammer turn speed omitted; Longshot's +25% range perk is moot under global targeting and awaits a replacement). `Start` plays the server-stored deck; the client never sends one.
- **Lobby:** shown in Lobby and Ended. Sprite HUD (design 1672x941, one UIScale; borderless icons with outlined labels, inline gold/gem counters, thin XP line, copper-framed deck slots): left grid Shop/Roll/Bag/Play/Codes/Daily, Quests, gear, bottom Gold (State), Gems, `LVL n` and the `x / y XP` bar (Meta; `MAX` at the top level) + 5-slot deck bar (a tap opens the Bag DICE tab). At join `Controllers/Preload` downloads every `*Sprites` asset and draws each once under the Hud's opaque loading message (DisplayOrder 7, over the Lobby's 6) until all report loaded (cap 20 s), so no menu opens blank. Bag opens the 4-tab Bag window (every tab live from Meta + Catalog; each `BagAction` reply is toasted in the Bag): MATERIALS/ITEMS pick a quantity 1..owned on the stepper and SELL it (price from the Catalog, shown in the description) or USE it (ITEMS with a Use text); LOCK/UNLOCK toggles a stack (padlock on the card, SELL/USE off); COSMETICS EQUIP/UNEQUIP one per slot (no in-game visuals yet, LOCK off); the loadout lives on the lobby hotbar (hold or drag a slot onto another to swap, in every menu); DICE UNEQUIP ALL clears it through `EquipDeck` (off when empty) and TEAMS opens five saved loadouts (`TeamAction` SAVE / LOAD), dice SELL off ("Copies level this die up"), and UPGRADE (gold, replaces LOCK) sends `UpgradeDie`; Roll, Shop, Daily, Quests, Play, Codes and the gear open their menus (below). The Codes popup draws at 2/3 of its former size on desktop and tablet (phones keep the old size; the X keeps a 44 px tap area). Panels are centred modals. Play: World 1 Trial + Solo or Co-op; the other worlds are shown locked; Infinite is deferred (the Trial itself is endless). Bag DICE: owned dice (rarity, level, equipped, spare copies "xN" on the card; stats Damage, Attack Speed and Burn Damage from the Catalog; the detail shows "Copies N / cost", "Copies N" at max level, the cost and cap from `Catalog.DieLevel`), then unowned playable dice ("Not owned"); Equip fills the next empty slot or lets the player choose a slot to replace when full. Selecting an equipped die shows Unequip. Solo and Co-op require five equipped dice per player. Ended shows a dismissable results card (result, wave, `+N Gold   +M XP`). Solo and two-player Co-op run in the same place. Both players opt in from the Play menu to start Co-op.
- **Settings** (gear; 20 settings in `src/shared/SettingsDefs.luau`, the one registry the client draws and the server cleans against): saved in the profile (`Settings`) and loaded from the first Meta that carries them (no toast, not sent back; Roblox's own Reduced Motion still forces ours ON); later Metas never overwrite the store. Player changes collect into one patch sent by `SaveSettings` 2 s after the last change and at once when the menu closes; a send the client refuses itself (throttle, one in flight) stays pending and retries, and a server rejection drops it. Shadows, UI Scale, Reduced Motion, Show FPS and Projectile Effects take effect (the short tabs, Gameplay 4 and Misc 5, use Audio's pitch and are centred); the other 15 are saved but labelled "coming soon" (Auto Skip Waves is never decided by the client; the audio volumes have no sounds yet).
- **Quests** (server-only `Config/Quests.luau`, each reward a `Rewards` bundle, *tune*): S1 finish a Trial run (RunsPlayed ≥ 1) 50 Gold + 10 Gems; S2 clear wave 5 (BestWave ≥ 5) 100 + 20; S3 clear wave 10 (TrialCleared) 250 + 50; D1 daily: defeat 150 enemies today 100 + 10; W1 weekly: defeat 1000 enemies this week 200 + 30; W2 weekly: finish 10 runs this week 300 + 30. One-time progress is derived from the stats only the run reward writes; the daily and W1 count server kills (deaths by damage, never breaches) of settled runs and W2 counts settled runs, added in the same run-id-gated settle as the reward. UTC day = `floor(os.time()/86400)` on the server and week = `floor((day + 3) / 7)` (starts Monday 00:00 UTC); effective day/week = `max(now-derived, stored)`, so a skewed clock never reopens a claimed day or week; the daily and weekly roll only at settle. Each quest pays once (per day for D1, per week for W1/W2).
- **Other claims** (all through `ClaimQuest` and `Rules/Claims`, rewards *tune*): **LOGIN** calendar (`Config/Daily.luau`, 7 bundles of Gold, Gems, XP Tomes, Gold Pouches, Fragments and starter die copies; Day 7 = 1000 Gold + 100 Gems + 2 XP Tomes, shown as a chest): claimable when today > `Login.LastDay`; the streak continues (`Streak % 7 + 1`) when LastDay was yesterday, else restarts at Day 1 (so Day 7 is followed by Day 1); pays `Days[streak]` and sets LastDay = today; a clock behind LastDay never pays. **Play milestones** P10, P20, P30, P40, P50, P100 (`Config/Milestones.luau`): once when BestWave ≥ wave; P10 pays 50 Gems + a Frost (Rare) die independently of the separate tutorial gift; every milestone is Reachable (runs are endless). **Achievements** A1–A5 (`Config/Achievements.luau`, Badge = a Quest Log sprite key): progress derived from RunsPlayed, TrialCleared, `Stats.Kills`, account level and different dice owned; A2, A4 and A5 also grant a cosmetic. **Mastery**: at settle every owned die of the match deck gains Mastery Xp = waves completed; level = `Progression.Level(Xp, Progression.Mastery)`; levels 5/10/15/20 pay `Progression.Mastery.Rewards` once per owned die.
- **Roll** (server-only `Config/Roll.luau`, *tune*): one pool `Standard`, 1 roll = 100 Gems, 10 rolls = 900 Gems; rarity weights in basis points Common 5889, Rare 3000, Epic 1000, Legendary 100, Mythic 10, Secret 1 (sum 10000); a rarity's dice are every `Config/Dice` entry of it, drawn uniformly; pity: the 80th roll in a row without a Legendary is one (the counter resets on any Legendary); Featured = Chrono, Astral, Forge. `Rules/Roll.Draw(pity, pool, count, rng)` is `count` single draws in a row with the counter carried through (ROLL 10 = ten single draws). `Roll.Buy` checks UnknownPool, UnknownPrice, Restricted (paid random items not allowed), Insufficient, then pays ONE `Rewards.Apply({Gems = -price, Dice = {[id] = n}})` (a new die arrives at Level 1, duplicates become copies) and, only after it landed, writes `RollPity[pool]` and `Stats.Rolls += count` with no yield between. The client sends only (pool, count); odds, prices, pity and the `Random` stay on the server. **PlayerPolicy** (`Services/PlayerPolicy.luau`) caches `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync(player).ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` per player (fetched in its own thread on join, pcall, 3 tries with 1 s / 2 s backoff, cleared on leave); unknown or failed = not allowed (fail closed), except in Studio where a failed lookup counts as allowed; the handler only reads the cache, and the answer re-pushes Meta. While a roll is in flight the Roll menu plays a PLACEHOLDER animation (neutral tumbling dice, `ROLLING...` label, buttons off, at least 0.6 s, then a 0.3 s reveal; none under Reduced Motion); the outcome still comes only from the server, a 10 s client timeout returns the buttons to normal, and a press while that lost request is still open toasts "Still waiting for the last roll". The Roll menu shows the Catalog's odds as true percentages (Bp / 100: 58.89 / 30 / 10 / 1 / 0.1 / 0.01%), the Catalog's prices, the pity bar `n / 80` from Meta and the featured dice (die art or the tinted roll glyph, with the die name and rarity below); ROLL 1 / ROLL 10 are on only when Meta.RollAllowed and Gems ≥ price (else "Not available in your region" / "Not enough Gems"); a `RollResult` replaces the featured dice with "YOUR ROLL" (one result centred, else the three rarest) until the menu closes and toasts "+1 Frost (new)" or "+10 dice (2 new)". EVENT shows "No event right now"; no rotation timer.
- **Shop** (server-only `Config/Shop.luau`, *tune*; ids 1..16 ASCII bytes): Gold/Gems offers `XpTome1` 1 XP Tome = 300 Gold, `Frag20` 20 Verdant Fragments = 100 Gold, `XpTome3` 3 XP Tomes = 40 Gems (rule, spec-tested: for every Inventory id Sell x count stays below the Gold paid for it, and nothing sells Gold for Gems or Robux; XP Tomes therefore have no Sell price, so Gems never turn into Gold); Robux offers `Starter` (1200 Gems + 1 R01 die + 2000 Gold, Limit 1 per account, 399) and gem packs `Gems400` (99), `Gems1200` (249), `Gems3500` (699) grant that many Gems. A Robux offer's `ProductId` is EMPTY until the owner provides the real developer-product ids; an offer without one is not Available (the client shows "Not available yet" and never prompts). No gamepasses (LUCK BOOST and AUTO ROLL dropped, VIP undefined): the Gamepasses and Shards sections show "Coming soon". `Rules/Shop.Buy(profile, offerId)` for the `Buy` remote: UnknownOffer, NotAvailable (a Robux offer never goes through Buy), SoldOut (Purchases >= Limit), then ONE `Rewards.Apply` of the price as a negative Gold or Gems counter plus the grant (Insufficient, Overflow leave everything unchanged), then `Purchases[id] += 1` for a limited offer. **Robux receipts** (`Services/ShopService`, the only Shop code that yields): `MarketplaceService.ProcessReceipt` answers NotProcessedYet when the player or profile is not here, the ProductId is unknown (warned) or the PurchaseId is malformed; if the PurchaseId is already in `Receipts` nothing is granted again; else in one no-yield block it runs `Rewards.Apply(grant)` (a paid receipt past an offer's Limit still grants, because the player was charged; the Limit only gates the client's prompt), `Purchases += 1` capped at the Limit for a limited offer, appends the id to `Receipts` (newest 100) and marks the profile changed; then `ProfileService.SaveNow` and PurchaseGranted ONLY if it saved, else NotProcessedYet (Roblox retries; the retry finds the id and only saves again), so a purchase is granted exactly once. `ShopService.StudioGrant(player, offerId)` (error unless `RunService:IsStudio()`; not a remote) runs the same code for a Robux offer with a fake PurchaseId `studio-<GUID>`; from the command bar call `game.ServerStorage.StudioGrant:Invoke(player, offerId)` (a Studio-only BindableFunction: the command bar's own `require` gets separate module copies with no loaded profiles). The Shop menu shows `Catalog.Shop` in one scrollable page. The page uses Gems (Meta) and Gold (State) to render the Starter hero (its real contents; "Not available yet" while unavailable, "Owned" once sold out) + the Gold/Gems offers as cards (Gold shows the coin, Gems the gem, Robux the Robux icon) and the gem packs. A Gold/Gems offer sends `Buy(id)` and is enabled only when the balance covers the price (replies toasted in the Shop); an available Robux offer only calls `PromptProductPurchase` (the server grants from the receipt).
- **Board** 5×3 (cells 1..15, row-major). **Summon:** costs `10 + 10*s` MP (`s` = successful summons this match: 10, 20, 30, …); places a pip-1 die of a random deck type in a random empty cell. **Merge** `from → to`: both occupied, same type, same pip, pip ≤ 6, `from ≠ to`; `from` is cleared, `to` becomes pip+1 with a type re-rolled from the deck. Pip 7 shows as **7★**.
- **MP** (match-only currency, formerly Energy): start 100 (exactly the first 4 summons: 10+20+30+40); a kill pays by wave band (`Balance.KillMp`, *tune*): 10 (waves 1–5), 20 (6–10), 30 (11–15), 40 (16–20), 50 (21+), Brute ×5, Boss ×10; no between-wave stipend.
- **Waves** are endless. Solo and two-player Co-op use the same historical HP curve per lane. Spawn timing and movement use the local formulas. The sources, balance multiplier, lane rules, and tutorial are documented in [Solo and Co-op difficulty](COOP_SCALING.md).
- **Cadence:** first wave starts 4 s after Start; each next wave 4 s after the previous one is cleared. When the next wave is a boss wave (every 5th), `BossAt` is set at the start of that prep (4 s warning) and moved to the Boss's actual spawn time when the wave starts (it is queued last), so the banner counts down until the Boss walks in.
- **Lives** 3; breach −1 (Grunt, Runner) or −2 (Brute, Boss). **Lost** at lives ≤ 0. Clearing wave 10 (`Balance.TrialWave`) sets TrialCleared and counts in `Stats.Wins` (no reward bonus); the run goes on.
- **Combat:** dice auto-attack, and the primary shot targets the living enemy **closest to the exit** (greatest path distance) anywhere on the path; there is no range limit (playtest feedback). `hitDamage = max(1, floor(base * perk * tier multiplier)) before flat bonuses`. Perks: Pebble ×1.05 vs Grunt; Hammer ×1.12 vs armored; Longshot ×0.9 vs Boss; Needle every 6th hit of that die instance +8 flat; Twinshot fires two shots at base damage 6 each. The second selects uniformly from all living enemies, including the front enemy. Cooldown does not bank while idle; max 2 attacks per die per tick.
- **Reward** at match end (Lost, or Aborted when the owner leaves or the server shuts down with the profile still loaded, settled exactly like a loss at that moment; not on error), exactly once per run id, as one all-or-nothing `Rewards.Apply` bundle (*tune*): Gold `5*wavesCompleted`, account Xp `10*wavesCompleted`, Verdant Fragments `wavesCompleted`, Gems on Random Dice co-op's card schedule (`Balance.WaveGems`, `Formulas.WaveGems`): per cleared wave 1, a Brute wave 2, a Boss wave 3 (the repeating 1, 1, 2, 1, 3 = 8 per 5 waves, 24 for 15), from wave 46 a plain wave 2 and a Brute or Boss wave 6. Zero parts are left out (a wave-0 loss pays nothing but still counts the run); any refusal (e.g. a counter at the 1e9 cap) changes nothing. Also BestWave, RunsPlayed, TrialCleared, `Stats` (Kills, Wins), the daily and weekly counters and the deck's Mastery Xp.
- **Speeds (*tune*):** Grunt 6, Runner 9, Brute 16/3, Boss 3 studs/s.
- **Dice visuals:** white cube with dark type-tinted pips on the top face in standard 1–6 layouts; 7 is one large gold star; type colour also on a band around the lower sides. Selected die lifts with a yellow outline; valid merge partners get a white outline.
- **Framing:** the path hugs the board and the camera is fitted so path, board and boss clear the HUD at 1235×739, 844×390 and 667×375 (dice ≈ 65 px desktop, ≈ 34 px phone).

## Server data model
- **MatchState** `{runId, rng, deck (own copy), kills, phase="Playing"|"Ended", result, mp, summons, lives, wave, wavesCompleted, waveState="Prep"|"Active", nextWaveAt, bossAt, queue, qi, nextSpawnAt, enemies, nextEnemyId, board[1..15] = Die|false, nextDieId, lastNow, rewardGranted, out}`. Lobby = no MatchState.
- **Die** `{id, type, pip, cd, hits}`. **Enemy** `{id, kind, hp, maxHp, speed, spawnAt, dist}`; `dist = speed * (now - spawnAt)`.
- **Profile v10** `{Version=10, Gold, Gems, Xp, BestWave, RunsPlayed, TrialCleared, LastRewardRunId, Dice={[id]={Level 1..7 (Progression.DieLevel.Max), Copies}}, Deck={5 ids}, Claimed={[questId]=true}, Daily={Day, Kills, Claimed={[questId]=true}}, Weekly={Week, Kills, Runs, Claimed={[questId]=true}}, Stats={Kills, Wins, Rolls}, Login={Streak 0..7, LastDay}, Milestones={[id]=true}, Achievements={[id]=true}, Mastery={[dieId]={Xp, Claimed={["5"]=true}}}, Redeemed={[codeId]=true}, Materials={[id]=n}, Items={[id]=n}, Cosmetics={Owned={[id]=true}, Equipped={[slot]=id}}, Settings={[settingId]=value}, Locked={[id]=true}, RollPity={[poolId]=0..Max−1}, Purchases={[offerId]=1..Limit} (limited Config/Shop offers only), Receipts={newest 100 Marketplace PurchaseIds, 1..64 bytes, deduped, oldest first}, profileRevision, sessionLease}`; counts are integers in [0, 1e9] (Materials and Items counts ≥ 1); nil → default; bad shape → Corrupt; newer version → NewerVersion (never repaired or overwritten). New profiles begin the tutorial with 500 Gems reserved for their first five rolls. The migration that originally introduced Gems still grants 50 Gems to legacy v3 saves. Sanitising keeps only known dice (the 5 starters are always present), resets an illegal Deck to the starter deck, keeps only `true` flags for known quests of the right kind (`Claimed`: neither daily nor weekly; `Daily.Claimed`: daily; `Weekly.Claimed`: weekly) and for milestone, achievement and code ids in their configs, and keeps only `Config/Inventory` ids with positive counts (Materials, Items) or `true` flags (Cosmetics); `Mastery` keeps entries for `Config/Dice` ids, whose `Claimed` set holds only the curve's milestone levels, keyed by the level as a string (DataStores and remotes drop sparse numeric keys; a numeric key still reads as claimed); `Settings` keeps `SettingsDefs.clean(def, stored[id])` for each registry key (a missing key is its default on the client); `Locked` keeps `true` flags for `Config/Inventory` Material and Item ids; `Cosmetics.Equipped` keeps a slot only when its id is owned and that cosmetic's `Slot` is the key; `RollPity` keeps, per `Config/Roll` pool, an integer in 0..Max−1 (else 0; unknown pools dropped) and `Stats.Rolls` is a count; the flag, inventory, lock, mastery and settings walks go over the config or registry, so a huge stored table there costs nothing. `MIGRATIONS[1]` (v1→v2) keeps every v1 field and adds the defaults; `MIGRATIONS[2]` (v2→v3) keeps every v2 field and starts `Redeemed` empty; `MIGRATIONS[3]` (v3→v4) keeps every v3 field, grants the 50 starter Gems and starts Xp, Materials, Items and Cosmetics empty (stray values dropped); `MIGRATIONS[4]` (v4→v5) keeps every v4 field and starts Stats, Weekly, Login, Milestones, Achievements and Mastery empty (stray values dropped); `MIGRATIONS[5]` (v5→v6) keeps every v5 field and starts Settings empty (stray value dropped); `MIGRATIONS[6]` (v6→v7) keeps every v6 field and starts Locked and Cosmetics.Equipped empty (stray values dropped); `MIGRATIONS[7]` (v7→v8) keeps every v7 field and starts RollPity (0 per pool) and Stats.Rolls (0) empty (stray values dropped); `MIGRATIONS[8]` (v8→v9) keeps every v8 field and starts Purchases and Receipts empty (stray values dropped); `MIGRATIONS[9]` (v9→v10) keeps every v9 field and clamps stored die levels 8–10 to 7 with no refund (no code path ever raised a level before v10, so none were paid for; from v10 on, a future cap cut must refund); the store writes the result back on load. Copies are spent by `UpgradeDie` (Level + 1); neither Level nor Copies has any combat effect yet (`Formulas` ignore the level). BALANCING's Gold cost per level is not implemented: a level-up costs spare copies only.

## Tick
`RunService.Heartbeat` in MatchService calls `Match.Step(state, workspace:GetServerTimeNow())` at 10 Hz, `dt` clamped to 0.25 s, inside `pcall` (error → result "Error", no reward). Order: wave advance → spawn → move + breach (lose check) → dice attacks → deaths + kill MP → wave clear / win.

**Enemy rendering:** server creates no enemy parts. Clients draw each enemy at `Layout.PositionAt(speed * (GetServerTimeNow() - spawnAt))` (exact because the MVP has no slows). HP, hits and deaths are server truth.

## Remotes (client sends intent only)
Every intent is a RemoteFunction (`InvokeServer`; no `InvokeClient` anywhere) returning `(ok: boolean, code: string?)`. Guard order: token bucket per player+remote → validator with **exact** arg count (pcall) → ready (profile loaded, sender is owner) → `seq` integer in [1, 2^31−1] and greater than the last accepted (consumed even if the rules reject) → handler (validates, then mutates, never yields, pcall).
Hard violations (RateLimited, BadArgs, BadSeq, and a client firing a server→client event) are logged (throttled) and kick at 20. Soft rejections (NotOwner, NotLoaded, WrongPhase, NoMP, BoardFull, IllegalMerge, BadDeck, UnknownDie, NotOwned, UpgradeDie's BadAmount, MaxLevel, UnknownQuest, NotComplete, AlreadyClaimed, Overflow, BagAction's UnknownItem, Insufficient, Locked, NotSellable, NotUsable, Roll's UnknownPool, UnknownPrice, Restricted, Buy's UnknownOffer, NotAvailable, SoldOut, and RedeemCode's Invalid, Expired, AlreadyRedeemed and its own RateLimited) are not counted.

- The client sends Summon at most once every 0.125 s and Merge once every 0.25 s. All intents share a monotonically increasing `seq`.
- The admin panel opens from the bottom-right button. Every `AdminAction(seq, action, userId, kind, id, quantity)` checks the sender's Roblox user ID against the server allowlist. Admins can grant or take Gold, Gems, XP, live match MP, dice, materials, items, and cosmetics. Revoking a die removes its copies and ownership and fills any equipped slot with a starter. Starter ownership supports the guaranteed deck. Offline edits require an existing profile with no active session lease. Reset restores starter defaults and retains paid receipt IDs to prevent repeat fulfillment. Resetting a player in this server closes their session so they can rejoin with the reset profile.

## Persistence
Live store `DD_Profiles_v1`; Studio uses `DD_Profiles_v1_Studio`, or the in-memory store when Studio has no API access (in-memory is only reachable when `RunService:IsStudio()`). Key `Profile_<userId>`. 120 s lease renewed by each save; LeaseHeld waits up to 130 s; LeaseLost/StaleRevision → profile forgotten, Phase "Failed", no further writes. Saves: match end (after reward), 60 s autosave, leave (release), BindToClose (3 attempts, 25 s). Owner leaving or shutdown mid-match aborts and settles like a loss before the release save. Load failure → no board, no rewards, nothing written.

## Attack VFX scale

Author Common projectiles around 0.18–0.22 studs in Blender, with impacts around 0.7–0.75 studs across at full size. `Projectiles.luau` applies the shared 3.5× game scale to the shot, streak, and impact geometry. At the match camera, start with a 0.63–0.77 stud shot, a streak no longer than about 5 studs, and an impact around 2.5 studs across. Check readability during normal play and aim to keep the impact within one ordinary enemy silhouette.
