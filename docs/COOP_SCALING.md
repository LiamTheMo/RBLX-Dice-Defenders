# Solo and Co-op difficulty

Dice Defenders uses one wave recipe per lane. Solo spawns one copy of the recipe. Two-player Co-op spawns the same recipe on two mirrored lanes that join before the exit. The lane length matches Solo. Both players share wave progress and lives. Each player has a separate board, deck, summon budget, and Power Up tiers. Every kill pays MP to both players.

## Reference and limits

The HP curve comes from first-hand measurements of Random Dice Defense v5.2.4 through wave 194. The measured sequence carries enemy HP progression from one wave to the next, ramps the per-enemy increase by wave band, replaces the first slot on miniboss waves, and uses a single stronger boss in place of the normal wave. Runners halve their slot's health before the value is converted to local HP. The v5.2.4 observer recorded deviations in high-wave boss HP, so those waves are projections. [Measured HP and early-wave examples](https://note.com/inkoman/n/ne18b17fb172f)

The developer's v8.9.2 release notes confirm a later Co-op rework and omit the formula. A guide from September 2024 shows a different boss schedule. Exact parity with the current game is unverified. [Official version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/random-dice-defense/id1462877149) · [Post-rework Co-op guide](https://w.atwiki.jp/truerandomdice/pages/21.html)

## Local balance

Both modes multiply the reference HP by 0.18 and round to the nearest whole number. This keeps the historical growth curve in proportion to the damage of Dice Defenders' starter dice. The wave-10 boss has 47,000 HP in the historical source and 8,460 HP per lane here. Local spawn timing, boss behavior, and run rewards use Dice Defenders' rules.

New profiles receive 500 tutorial Gems reserved for five free, distinct starter rolls. The game equips the resulting dice. Clearing wave 10 in the Solo tutorial pauses progression for the player's choice to end or keep playing. The separate gift grants one random Epic die, 500 regular Gems, and five Dice Shards. One shard converts into one spare copy of a die the player owns.
